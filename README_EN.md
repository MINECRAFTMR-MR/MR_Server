##📝 Introduction
 
- The MR server has been refined and optimized for nearly seven months since its release
- It has accumulated nearly 200 downloads on MineBBS
- Comes with more than 80 built-in plugins to enrich entertaining gameplay
 
##🍉 Gameplay Types
 
- Built-in mods such as CE and Mythical Creatures
- Extra add-ons include Mucus Technology and its successor Pylon mod
- Diverse gameplay systems are available in-game: laid-back survival, power generation, magic, firearms, farming, combat and more
 
##🎗 Other Matching Servers
 
- Supporting mini-game exclusive servers including **MR_Game** and **MR_Pestcan**
- Both servers support one-click server creation and one-click deployment, with in-depth performance optimization
